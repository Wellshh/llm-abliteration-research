## §14 budget re-estimation — methodology (math ready, real numbers gated)

`reestimate_phase_budgets(plans, prefill_tokens_per_s, decode_forward_per_s, fixed_overhead_per_run_s, pre_admission_audit_s)` computes, per phase:
- `compute_s_per_run = fixed_overhead + prefill_tokens/prefill_tps + decode_tokens/decode_fps`
- **`charged_compute_s`** = `n_runs × compute_s_per_run` — admitted-worker wall (load+compute+idle), **excludes** the ≥60s pre-admission audit (this is what `PhaseBudget` charges; RES-04).
- **`true_occupancy_s`** = `n_runs × (compute_s_per_run + attempts_per_run × pre_admission_audit_s)` — real GPU-occupancy wall a §14 cap should be checked against.
- Compares both to `PHASE_CAP_HOURS` (P0 5h, P1 10h, P2 25h, P3 40h, P4 20h, P5 20h; main total 120h).

The two totals are reported separately and must not be conflated. `fixed_overhead_per_run_s` should be the **measured** model-load+tokenize+anchor overhead (T04 observed ~12.5s/run, RES-02), not assumed.

**Worked example (ILLUSTRATIVE ONLY — placeholder rates, NOT measured):** with `prefill_tokens_per_s=8000`, `decode_forward_per_s=40`, `fixed_overhead=12.5`, `audit=60`, a P1 plan of `n_runs=200, prefill_tokens=300, decode_tokens=16`:
- compute/run = 12.5 + 300/8000 + 16/40 = 12.5 + 0.0375 + 0.4 = **12.94s**; charged = 200×12.94 = **2588s (0.72h)**; occupancy = 200×(12.94+60) = **14588s (4.05h)**; P1 cap 10h → within cap. (The `tests.test_resource_profile` suite verifies this arithmetic deterministically; the placeholder rates here are **not** measurements.)

**The real re-estimate is GATED:** `prefill_tokens_per_s` / `decode_forward_per_s` / allocator peak must come from a `real_gpu_measurement` profile produced by the gated `--device cuda:0` run. Until then, `phase0_construct_admission`'s "prefill/decode-separated resource re-estimation missing" item stays open, and `--budget-only` will refuse a non-illustrative re-estimate.
